abstract: > 
  Symbolic Dynamics is a part of the theory of dynamical systems, with strong
  connections to ergodic theory and information theory. This course will develop
  the fundamentals of symbolic dynamics, with a focus on classification problems
  for shifts of finite type and sofic shifts and applications of symbolic
  dynamics to modelling smooth dynamical systems. The course textbooks will
  be: An Introduction to Symbolic Dynamics and Coding, Second Edition, 2021,
  by Douglas Lind and Brian Marcus and Equilibrium States and the Ergodic
  Theory of Anosov Diffeomorphisms by Rufus Bowen (with updates and
  corrections by Jean-Rene Chazottes, 2017)
